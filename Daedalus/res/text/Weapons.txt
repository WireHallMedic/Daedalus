@AUTOGUN
Autoguns fire a rapid series of projectiles. While the damage of each is lower than single-shot weapons, the cumulative effect is greater.

@SCATTERGUN
Scatterguns project a cone of energy, allowing the weilder to hit several targets with a single shot. However, the energy intensity drops off with distance.

@BOLTGUN
Boltguns concentrate all their energy into a single slug of energy, which impacts the target. Boltguns have better range than other comparable weapons.

@PLASMA CANNON
Plasma cannons fire an unstable ball of energy which detonates on impact, causing significant damage over an area. The extreme amount of energy per shot means longer charge times and fewer maximum shots per charge.

@BEAM CANNON
Beam cannons fire a powerful ray of concentrated energy, which affects everything in a line. The extreme amount of energy per shot means longer charge times and fewer maximum shots per charge.

@BATON
A combat baton is a mace-like club which projects an energy field around the weighted end. As it requires much less power than if the energy were to be projected over a distance, combat batons do not need to recharge between strikes.

@SCAVENGED
This weapon is built from scavenged and repurposed parts, and is less effective than a manufactured version.

@+RANGE
This weapon is modified for improved range.

@+CAPACITY
This weapon has additional capacitors, allowing for a higher maximum capacity.

@+ROF
This weapon is modified to fire additional projectiles per burst, with no loss in individual power.

@+HIGHEST DAMAGE
This weapon is modified to increase its damage output.

@+DAMAGE
This weapon is modified to additionally deal %s damage.

@+RECHARGE
This weapon is modified with lower gauge conduits, allowing it to charge faster.
