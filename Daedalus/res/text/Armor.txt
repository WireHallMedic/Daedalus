@HARNESS
While it doesn't provide any physical protection, a utility harness contains a microreactor to power weapons and shields, as well as several ports for gadgets.

@SCOUT
Scout armor trades some of the typical physical protection for additional gadget slots. As with all armors, it has a microreactor for charging weapons, shields, and gadgets.

@STANDARD
Body armor which provides protection against physical attacks. As with all armors, it has a microreactor for charging weapons, shields, and gadgets.

@ASSAULT
Assault armor provides heavy protection against physical attacks, at the expense of some gadget ports. As with all armors, it has a microreactor for charging weapons, shields, and gadgets.

@SCAVENGED
This armor is built from scavenged and repurposed parts, and is less effective than a manufactured version.

@+REINFORCED
This armor has been strengthened to provide additional protection from physical attacks.

@+INSULATED
Special materials used in the construction of this armor provide protection against energy attacks.

@+ENGINEERS
This armor has been crafted with additional gadget ports.

@+ELITE
This expertly-crafted armor has additional protection against all types of damage.
