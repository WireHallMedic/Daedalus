@SHIELD
Shields project a field of ablative energy around the user, which recharges over time.

@HEAVY
Shields project a field of ablative energy around the user, which recharges over time. Heavy shields can absorb more damage than others, but have a longer delay before they begin recharging.

@QUICK
Shields project a field of ablative energy around the user, which recharges over time. Quick-charge shields recharge with less delay and more quickly than other types, but have a lower total capacity.

@SCAVENGED
This shield is built from scavenged and repurposed parts, and is less effective than a manufactured version.

@+REACTIVE
This shield is modified to begin recharging more quickly.

@+HIGH CAPACITY
This shield is modified to project a stronger energy field around the user.

@+OVERCLOCKED
This shield has been modified to charge more quickly.
